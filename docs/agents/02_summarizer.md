# Summarizer Agent ("The Archivist")

**Component:** `rag_pipeline/summary_generator.py`
**Trigger:** Post-indexing (`setup_summaries.py`)
**Default Model:** `ministral-8b`

The Summarizer builds a "Big Picture" view of the data. It does not look at raw messages but processes the *Narrative Summaries* generated by the Enricher.

## Hierarchy

1.  **Conversation Summaries:** Overview of the entire relationship with a person.
    *   *Output:* Main topics, relationship dynamic, notable events.
2.  **Period Summaries:** Monthly breakdowns of the relationship.
    *   *Output:* Topics, mood, summary of that month.

## Prompt Strategy

Uses two distinct prompts:
- `CONVERSATION_SUMMARY_PROMPT`: Focuses on long-term trends and relationship dynamics.
- `PERIOD_SUMMARY_PROMPT`: Focuses on specific events and mood of the specific timeframe.
